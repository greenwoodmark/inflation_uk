US CPURNSA PCA Diagnostics

How many empirical principal components explain variation in the six shadow-posterior drivers?

Frame-aware analysis. The July 14 publication reseed changes the meaning of the relative driver buckets. PCA is therefore calculated separately within each coordinate frame; the cross-reseed transition is not treated as a market move.

Current frame: explained variance

Natural units are the primary measure. Standardized units give each driver equal marginal volatility before PCA.

Current-frame expanding history

Each point recomputes PCA using all available posterior states in the current frame up to that date. The series starts after enough states exist for a six-driver covariance estimate.

Frame comparison

Method and limitations

    PCA is a linear variance-concentration diagnostic, not a causal model and not posterior uncertainty. Independent-component analysis (ICA) is not used here: with short, correlated posterior paths it is unstable and does not provide a comparable “variance explained” measure.
