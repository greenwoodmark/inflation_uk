US CPURNSA PCA Diagnostics

How many empirical principal components explain variation in the remaining projected CPI nodes?

Reseed-aware rolling analysis. PCA uses a 60-day window of posterior curves. Projected nodes are aligned by absolute reference month, so a published front node is not compared with a forecast node after reseeding. Valid states before and after a reseed remain in the window; the reseed transition itself is not treated as a market move.

Method and limitations

    PCA is a linear variance-concentration diagnostic, not a causal model and not posterior uncertainty. Independent-component analysis (ICA) is not used here: with short, correlated posterior paths it is unstable and does not provide a comparable “variance explained” measure.
