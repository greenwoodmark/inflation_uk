TIP vs TLT options relative value

This project studies relative value between TIP, the iShares TIPS Bond ETF, and TLT, the iShares 20+ Year Treasury Bond ETF. It combines daily one-month GS Marquee volatility smiles with GH5 distribution fits and high-frequency ETF returns. The research goal is to model joint TIP/TLT behaviour, including Student-t copula dependence across horizons, then evaluate long-TIP/short-TLT 10-delta put and call trades under explicit risk-neutral and real-world assumptions. The chart below shows the latest raw and GH5-fitted implied-volatility smiles, while hover metadata records fit diagnostics and the market-value-weighted effective-duration proxy from the latest official iShares holdings snapshots for comparison and ongoing monitoring.
